    BEFORE YOU EXPLORE

    A few things worth knowing.

    The boundaries are part of the project.

    Does opening this site scan anything?

    No. The site reads existing assessment records. Capturing scan evidence is a separate, authorised lab activity.

    Does the AI decide what is most urgent?

    No. A published, deterministic formula produces the scores. The optional local model explains the recorded findings and offers suggestions for human review.

    Are the example results real?

    Check the label beside the example. Synthetic examples illustrate the method; they are not findings about real systems. Recorded examples are only as strong as their source evidence.

    Does a high score mean the system was hacked?

    No. A priority score is a reason to investigate a weakness, not evidence of a successful attack.

    Does this prove context gives better priorities?

    Not by itself. The research compares rankings with independent expert judgement. A demonstration or a working interface does not establish that result.
