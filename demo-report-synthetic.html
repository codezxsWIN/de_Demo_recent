4. Methodology

Score = CVSS 3.1 Environmental score (Modified metrics and C/I/A Requirements auto-filled from the inferred context) × 10, multiplied by a threat factor 0.5 + 0.5 × EPSS percentile when EPSS data exists (otherwise unscored: environmental × 10 only). A CISA KEV listing (confirmed exploitation) forces the threat factor to 1.0, adds 10 and, only when the host is internet-facing, applies a floor of 90. Findings without a CVE use the tool's native severity table. The language model is not used in this prototype; every reason is a deterministic sentence built from the same facts shown above.
