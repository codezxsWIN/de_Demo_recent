TLS management comparison

Loopback port 8443 uses an expired certificate and unauthenticated management. Port 8444 provides a certificate within its validity period and an authenticated comparison.

The comparison certificate is self-signed; validity dates do not establish browser trust. Inventory alone does not validate TLS or access controls.
